# How to download from Baidu Pan (pan.baidu.com): tutorials in English

Short, practical guides for people outside China who received a Baidu Pan (Baidu Netdisk) share link and cannot get the files: no Baidu account, no Chinese phone number, a client in Chinese, or a download that crawls.

The guides use [PanPull](https://panpull.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tutorial), a website that downloads a Baidu share for you. You paste the link, see the size and the price, and download in your browser. Shares up to 150 MB are free.

## Quick start

1. Open the [PanPull link checker](https://panpull.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tutorial#link-checker).
2. Paste your `pan.baidu.com` link and its extraction code.
3. Look at the file count, the total size and the price.
4. Sign in with an email address, pay if the order is not free, and download.
