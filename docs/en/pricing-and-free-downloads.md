# Baidu Pan download cost: free options, SVIP and pay-per-share prices

Downloading from Baidu Pan is free if you have a Baidu account, but it is slow. To go faster you pay either Baidu or a service that downloads the share for you. This page compares the options and shows PanPull's prices with examples.

## Your options

| Option | Cost | What you need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Baidu account | Free | A Baidu account, usually the desktop app, and patience: about 2 hours per GB |
| Baidu SVIP membership | A subscription paid in Chinese yuan | A Baidu account and a way to pay on Baidu's membership page |
| PanPull | Per share, by size. Up to 150 MB is free | An email address. PayPal or a card for paid orders |

## PanPull prices

Prices as of November 2026, in US dollars. The [pricing page](https://panpull.com/pricing?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tutorial) always shows the current ones.

- Up to 150 MB in total: **free**. Each account gets 5 free orders in any 30 days.
- First GB: **$0.98**.
- Each additional GB: **$0.48**.
- Large shares have a maximum price:

| Total size | Maximum price |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 50 GB | $4.80 |
| Up to 150 GB | $9.80 |
| Up to 300 GB | $19.80 |
| Up to 500 GB | $29.80 |
| Up to 1 TB | $49.80 |

One GB means 1,000,000,000 bytes here. A started GB counts as a full one, so 3.2 GB is billed as 4 GB.

## How to see your exact price

Run your share link through the [link checker](https://panpull.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tutorial#link-checker). The result shows the file count, the total size and the exact price, and you decide after that. Checking is free and does not need an account. A quote is valid for 30 minutes; checking again gives you a new one.

## Paying

- Payment goes through PayPal: with your PayPal balance, or with a debit or credit card.
- You pay once per order. There is no subscription.
- You get your money back when a paid order cannot be delivered. See the [refund policy](https://panpull.com/refund?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tutorial).

More on the PanPull blog: [Baidu download pricing: how your quote is calculated](https://panpull.com/blog/understanding-your-download-price?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tutorial).
