# Baidu account without a Chinese phone number: can you sign up from abroad?

Baidu will not create an account without a mobile number that it can text. That stops many people outside China before they download anything. This page helps you decide in two minutes: is it worth trying to sign up, or can you skip the account?

## Step 1: is your country code offered?

Baidu has a sign-up page for users abroad at `passport.baidu.com/v2/?reg&overseas=1`. On 31 October 2026 we counted 161 country codes in its list.

| Offered | Missing |
| --- | --- |
| United States and Canada, United Kingdom, Switzerland, Norway, Japan, South Korea, Vietnam, Brazil, Mexico, Australia | All EU countries, including Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Poland, Portugal and Ireland |

To check your own country, open the page and click **+86** on the left of the first field. The list that opens shows every code Baidu takes.

- Your code is missing: you cannot sign up with your number. Go to step 3.
- Your code is there: go to step 2.

## Step 2: what an account gives you, and what it does not

| With a free Baidu account you can | You still cannot |
| --- | --- |
| Log in on the share page | Read the site in English: it stays Chinese |
| Save shared files to your own Netdisk | Download big files or folders in the browser: Baidu sends you to its desktop app |
| Start downloads | Download fast: free accounts are limited to roughly 100 to 170 KB/s |

Two more things to know before you try:

- Even with a listed country, the text message sometimes does not come. Internet-based (VoIP) numbers are the usual victims.
- Accounts for sale and rented numbers are a bad idea. Whoever controls the phone number controls the account.

## Step 3: choose

| Your situation | Best route |
| --- | --- |
| You need the files from one link, once | Skip the account. Use the steps in [How to download without a Baidu account](download-without-account.md) |
| Your country code is missing | Skip the account |
| You will use Baidu Pan regularly and your code is offered | Sign up. The PanPull blog translates the Chinese form field by field: [Baidu Netdisk sign-up outside China](https://panpull.com/blog/baidu-netdisk-sign-up-outside-china?utm_source=github&utm_medium=tutorial) |
